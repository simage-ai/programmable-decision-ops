# 1 Scope

These sheets record the design parameters of the three candidate configurations that the range model uses. All figures are synthetic and illustrative; they are not estimates for any real vehicle.

# 2 Vehicle A: current production derivative

The fuel system of configuration A holds a usable fuel capacity of 40 gal.

Over the mixed-terrain duty cycle, configuration A is rated at a fuel economy of 8.0 mi/gal at gross vehicle weight.

# 3 Vehicle B: extended-range hybrid

The fuel system of configuration B holds a usable fuel capacity of 45 gal, including the auxiliary tank.

Over the mixed-terrain duty cycle, configuration B is rated at a fuel economy of 8.0 mi/gal at gross vehicle weight, with the hybrid system in charge-sustaining mode.

# 4 Vehicle C: low-cost commercial platform

The fuel system of configuration C holds a usable fuel capacity of 47.5 gal.

Over the mixed-terrain duty cycle, configuration C is rated at a fuel economy of 8.0 mi/gal at gross vehicle weight.

# 5 Notes

Fuel economy ratings come from the supplier's duty-cycle simulation and are not yet confirmed by test.
