# 1 Purpose

This report gives the phase 1 reliability growth test results for the three candidate light tactical vehicle configurations. All figures are synthetic and illustrative; they are not estimates for any real vehicle.

# 2 Test conditions

Each test article completed 2,000 miles on the mixed-terrain endurance course at gross vehicle weight, in eight-hour shifts. Mission reliability is the probability of completing a 100-mile mission without an essential function failure, estimated from the failures observed with a Crow-AMSAA growth model.

# 3 Results

## 3.1 Vehicle A

Vehicle A, the current production derivative, recorded three essential function failures, all in the cooling system. Its demonstrated mission reliability is 0.97.

## 3.2 Vehicle B

Vehicle B, the extended-range hybrid, recorded two essential function failures, both in the hybrid power electronics. Its demonstrated mission reliability is 0.98.

## 3.3 Vehicle C

Vehicle C, the low-cost commercial platform, recorded six essential function failures across the suspension and the driveline. Its demonstrated mission reliability is 0.94, below the 0.95 threshold.

## 3.4 Summary

| Vehicle | Miles | Failures | Mission reliability |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 2,000 | 3 | 0.97 |
| B | 2,000 | 2 | 0.98 |
| C | 2,000 | 6 | 0.94 |

# 4 Limitations

Phase 1 articles are pre-production builds. Growth estimates assume the corrective actions identified during the test are carried into the production configuration.
